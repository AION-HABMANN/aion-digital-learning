# Task 1 · Route 1 · UX Analysis File (Levels 1 and 2)

## The case: EduPath, where different users fail in different ways

EduPath is a learning platform with three kinds of users. Learners drop out because they lack time. Teachers find the platform too complicated. The organisations that buy the courses complain about low usage. You are part of the UX team, and management wants you to understand each group before it decides what to build.

| What you have | The limits | How the task runs |
|---|---|---|
| Five touchpoints of the platform (below) and eight research notes about learners (Block 1.1).<br>A scenario for a journey (optional Block 1.2) and six notes about teachers and organisations (optional Block 2.1).<br>Nine measures, each with a cost and weeks (Block 2.2). | **Budget:** €45,000 (Case assumption)<br>**Time:** 3 months (13 weeks, Case assumption)<br>Resources are limited. | Two Core blocks, about 30 minutes:<br>1. Block 1.1: sort eight notes into persona fields and write the learner persona.<br>2. Block 2.2: choose four of nine measures, rate them, order them, and name the pain point each answers.<br>Three more blocks (about 20 minutes) are Optional. |

:::note Case assumption
The brief says: learners drop out due to lack of time, teachers find the platform too complicated, the organisation complains about low usage, a working professional with little time who wants to continue their education. Everything else is made up for this exercise: the research notes, the numbers in them, the €45,000, the 13 weeks, and the costs and weeks of the measures.
:::
