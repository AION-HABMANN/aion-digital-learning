# Task 1 · Route 1 · UX Analysis File (Levels 1 and 2)

## The case: NavLearn, a platform learners cannot find their way in

NavLearn is a learning platform with many courses. Users cannot find content, they rarely interact with what they do find, and their motivation is declining. A first analysis points to a poor information architecture and to a lack of activation. The menus have many entries in no clear order, the content is complex, and users lose interest quickly. You are part of the UX team, time is limited, and you are asked to work out what is wrong and which measures are worth funding.
