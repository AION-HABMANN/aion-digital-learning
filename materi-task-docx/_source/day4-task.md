# Task 1 · Route 1 · UX Analysis File (Levels 1 and 2)

## The case: MotivaLearn, a platform with low user retention

MotivaLearn is a learning platform whose learners start courses and then drop out. Learners call the content "boring", and nothing on the platform shows them that they are making progress. You are part of the UX team. Management wants to know what really motivates learners, and which measures are worth funding.

| What you have | The limits | How the task runs |
|---|---|---|
| Two platforms in outline: Platform A (a benchmark) and Platform B (MotivaLearn today), with eight facts (Block 1.1).<br>Three engagement options (optional Block 1.2) and six possible causes (optional Block 2.1).<br>Nine measures, each with a cost and weeks (Block 2.2). | **Time:** 2 months (8 weeks)<br>**Budget:** €40,000 (Case assumption: the plan only says "limited")<br>Users drop out early. | Two Core blocks, about 30 minutes:<br>1. Block 1.1: sort eight facts, say which platform is more motivating, write three principles of motivation.<br>2. Block 2.2: choose four of nine measures, rate them, order them.<br>Three more blocks (about 20 minutes) are Optional. |

:::note Case assumption
The brief says: learners start courses but drop out, the content is perceived as boring, no visible progress, a limited budget, two months. Platform A has a progress bar, small successes visible and short tasks; Platform B has long content, no feedback and unclear goals. Everything else is made up for this exercise: the drawings, the €40,000, and the costs and weeks of the measures.
